# Pre-registration: does the gap-expander capacity warning explain the outer duplication?

**Written 2026-09-01, before any arm is fitted.**

## Why a fourth hypothesis is worth testing when three failed

`reports/the_outer_boundary_is_configured_not_data.md` says a fourth explanation offered without a
test would be worth no more than the three before it. This one differs in kind: it is **not my
inference**. `spiral_helpers.py:1072` emits it, and every fit we have run prints it:

```
WARNING: shell_outer_winding_idx 130 requires model_gap_expander_num_windings >= 133,
         got model_gap_expander_num_windings 130; increase model_gap_expander_num_windings
         or lower shell_outer_winding_idx
```

Both values are villa **defaults** (`config.py:287` and `:489`) and neither is overridden in our
runs, so this fires on any default-config fit. The requirement is `min_gap = idx + 3`, so the model's
gap expander falls exactly **3 windings short** of the range the dense and regularisation losses
sample to.

Our duplication concentrates at w124 to w129, with the top pairs at w126 to w129. The outermost few
windings. That correspondence is what makes this worth an arm.

## The verification comes FIRST this time

All three previous verification conditions were mis-specified, and one cost 90 minutes of GPU on an
arm that could not have shown anything. So before the full fit:

**A 100-step smoke fit with `model_gap_expander_num_windings = 133`. The warning must disappear from
its log.** The warning is emitted from the config, early, so 100 steps is enough. If it still fires,
the manipulation does not do what I think and **no full fit is run**.

This observable demonstrably responds to this input: the warning text is generated by comparing these
two config values directly.

## Arm, if the smoke check passes

**GAP133**: identical to `baseline01` except `"model_gap_expander_num_windings": 133`. Everything
else default, including both spacing weights and `shell_outer_winding_idx = 130`.

## Predictions, fixed now

1. The warning is absent from the GAP133 log (checked at smoke stage).
2. **Full-fit gap>=2 falls below 0.0897%**, the bottom of the honest range 0.0897 to 0.1042%. If the
   shortfall is the cause, giving the model the capacity it asks for should reduce the duplication
   concentrated in exactly those outermost windings.
3. If gap>=2 falls, **the median wmax of the remaining cells should move inward** from 126.

## Decision rule

* gap>=2 **below 0.0897%** AND median wmax below 126: **supported**, the capacity shortfall
  contributes to the outer duplication.
* gap>=2 **inside 0.0897 to 0.1042%**: **null**, the shortfall is not the cause, and this is the
  fourth explanation to fail.
* gap>=2 **above 0.1042%**: the change made duplication worse, which would need reporting as such
  and would not support the hypothesis.
* satisfied area outside 0.8382 to 0.8404: fit quality moved too, and the result is confounded.

## Limits

One fit, one seed, one ROI. The warning says the capacity is short; it does not say what the
consequence is, and "the losses sample past the model's range" is my reading of the code rather than
documented behaviour. A null would leave the outer concentration unexplained for the fourth time,
and I would stop rather than propose a fifth.
