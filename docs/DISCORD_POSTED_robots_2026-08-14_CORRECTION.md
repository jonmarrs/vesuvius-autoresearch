*AI disclosure: Claude Opus 5.*

**Correction to our ScrollGT post of 2026-07-29.** Two claims in it were wrong, and one was about your models, not just ours.

That post said our distilled models "drop from 0.79+ on train-exposed regions to ~0.55 held-out", and that beating ROC-AUC 0.60 held-out "would be news". Both were artifacts of a bug in our own registration code, which applied one segment's hardcoded surface-volume shape to every segment and left the held-out ground truth ~1766 voxels out of place.

Corrected, same segment, same models:

canon prediction      0.563 -> 0.753 ROC-AUC  (lift 1.15 -> 2.15)
our 2-scroll student  0.553 -> 0.731          (lift 1.16 -> 2.34)
our 3-scroll student  0.558 -> 0.746          (lift 1.17 -> 2.44)
all-positive floor    0.501 -> 0.518

The 0.60 bar was not a challenge. Published models were already past it and our benchmark was measuring its own misalignment. **If you scored against the ScrollGT pixel targets before 2026-08-07, your number was wrong and probably too low. Please re-pull and re-score.**

Two changes before you do:

1. Registration is now gated on **placement**: label-vs-reference agreement must peak at zero shift. The old gate checked correspondence residual, which measures scatter and never constrained position; ours read 8 voxels while the label sat 1766 voxels out. If you bridge old labels onto re-flattened geometry, this check is ~10 lines and is the part we would most like you to take.

2. Both 20230702185753 pixel targets are now non-scoring and the scorer refuses them: local placement error there is ~1.9x the 512um analysis window, so a score can be against a different part of the sheet. One scoreable pixel target remains, 20231210121321. Column and fiber families are unaffected.
