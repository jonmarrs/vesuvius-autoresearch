Does villa's metric satisfy a REAL traced patch?

Every number in the winding-blindness report is measured on a synthetic patch
placed exactly on a winding. This scores villa's unmodified function on real
traced geometry instead, in the umbilicus-centred frame, with no synthetic
patch involved.

=== The scale problem, which cannot be resolved, only stated ===
  The synthetic patch is 12x16 cells over about 22 x 66 voxels: 165 quads in a
  small area, because its cells sit 2.0 and 4.4 voxels apart. Real traced patches
  are sampled at about 20 voxels per cell, so a real window matches extent or quad
  count, never both.

   window        cells     quads   extent (vox)
  ----------------------------------------------------------
   synthetic     12x16       165   ~22 x 66
   extent-matched 2x4           3   ~20 x 60
   quad-matched  12x16       165   ~220 x 300

=== Does a whole-winding displacement move the score on REAL geometry? ===
  The 0.5 and 5.5 rows are the control: if they were also zero, this construction
  could not move the score and the zeros at 1.0 and 2.0 would mean nothing.

  extent-matched (2x4 cells, 60 windows)
     displacement (windings)   max |delta|   share of windows changed
    --------------------------------------------------------------------
                     0.5        1.0000                      48%  <- control
                     1.0        0.0000                       0%
                     2.0        0.0000                       0%
                     5.5        1.0000                      48%  <- control

  quad-matched (12x16 cells, 36 windows)

    --------------------------------------------------------------------
                     0.5        0.1576                      92%  <- control
                     1.0        0.0000                       0%
                     2.0        0.0000                       0%

=== Verdict on the pre-registered rule ===
  ⚠ Only 21.7% of real extent-matched windows are satisfied at dr=12.81, below the pre-registered 50%.
  villa's metric rejects most real patches at this scale on their own merits,
  displaced or not. The exact algebra of the report still holds, but it
  describes a reference condition most real windows do not meet, and the report
  must carry that qualification.

Limits. dr is swept over [11.32, 12.81, 16.74] rather than fitted per patch, because the real spacing varies 11.32 to 16.74 across shards and a patch scored against a spacing it does not have would fail for the wrong reason. A per-patch fit is the follow-up. Windows are pooled with a per-patch cap, not a global quota.
