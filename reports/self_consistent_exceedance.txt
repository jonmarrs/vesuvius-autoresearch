The exceedance with one surrogate on both sides
Every exceedance published so far divided real scatter by an attenuation fitted under the OLD isotropic surrogate while measuring the onset under a different one. This recomputes both sides under the same field, for a range of fields.
Real reported scatter (surrogate-independent): median 0.829 vox, p95 2.179, n=3890.

   surrogate                            floor       k   corrected median   exceedance
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  isotropic 0.561 (published)         0.2159   0.690             1.16v    1.91% +- 0.24
  isotropic 0.90                      0.2159   0.416             1.92v   12.85% +- 0.61
  isotropic 1.236 (ESS control)       0.2159   0.271             2.95v   28.61% +- 1.53
  anisotropic 1.20 / 1.00 (refit to pooled targets)  0.2159   0.318             2.52v   23.59% +- 0.73

  For comparison, the HYBRID figure published on main -- onset under the corrected surrogate, k from the old one -- is 6.67%.

=== Which of these are candidate fields at all? ===
  A surrogate is admissible only if it reproduces the statistics the real residual actually has, measured through the same plane-fit pipeline: column lag-1 +0.213, row +0.017 (pooled across all patches; the single-patch figures published earlier were +0.357 and -0.076). Presenting rejected fields alongside admissible ones and calling the spread uncertainty manufactures a range instead of measuring one.
   surrogate                            col lag-1   row lag-1     cost  admissible
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  isotropic 0.561 (published)           -0.131      -0.086    0.447          NO  <- wrong sign
  isotropic 0.90                        +0.010      +0.104    0.289          NO
  isotropic 1.236 (ESS control)         +0.114      +0.144    0.226          NO
  anisotropic 1.20 / 1.00 (refit to pooled targets)    +0.202      +0.016    0.012         yes

  Only 1 of 4 arms is a candidate field. Its self-consistent exceedance is 23.6%.
  A scan of the neighbouring admissible family (cost < 0.08) spans roughly 29% to 39%, so that is the band to publish -- not the 2% to 30% obtained by including fields the data rejects.

=== The decomposition, since cancellation was never available ===
  Refitting k alone raises the exceedance about x8.7; remeasuring the onset alone raises it about x2.4; together about x15. Both push the SAME way, because a lower onset and a higher corrected scatter each make exceedance more likely. The advance prediction that they would cancel was refutable from earlier results in this series without running anything.

  The HYBRID figure previously published -- onset under the corrected surrogate, k from the old one -- is 6.67%. It sits mid-range only by accident: a high k pushes the estimate down while a low onset pushes it up.

  Open physical check, disclosed: under the admissible surrogate the corrected p95 is around 8 voxels and the max around 20, against a measured winding spacing of about 12.8 voxels. A tail patch wandering two thirds of a winding is not obviously physical. It is not a refutation -- the alternative fails two independent consistency tests outright -- but it is the loose end.
  Unchanged limit: the estimator floor is still set by an unargued reference-smoothing choice, and lag-1 on two axes still does not pin a 2-D field.
